# Business Intelligence & AI Solutions Architect

I design connected BI and AI systems, from governed data infrastructure to stakeholder-facing solutions, and the agentic engineering workflows used to build them.

My career began in client consulting and commercial and research analytics. Power BI, Microsoft Fabric and Power Platform led me into BI engineering. Today I work across those layers as an architect, connecting stakeholders' needs with technical choices to drive business outcomes, while keeping future maintainability in mind.

At Mastercard, I designed and continue to evolve a dynamic departmental analytics infrastructure. I also coordinate the department’s Copilot Studio environment, building stakeholder-facing agents connected to enterprise knowledge and Fabric Data Agents through workflows and APIs.

Alongside this, I build reusable, specialist-agent GitHub Copilot workspaces for Fabric and Power Platform, plus emerging agent-led patterns for repeatable app development. The objective throughout is useful solutions that can be built, operated and improved over time.

## What I build

**Stakeholder-facing BI solutions**  
End-to-end, multi-platform (Fabric/Power Platform) solutions: from automating data processes to building solutions that allow stakeholders to read and write data, while leadership can monitor the business.

**Stakeholder-facing AI solutions**  
Copilot Studio agents connected to Fabric Data Agents to make enterprise information (business knowledge and data) accessible through purposeful AI experiences.

**Agentic engineering systems**  
Multi-agent development workspaces for Fabric and Power Platform, alongside experiments with repeatable agent-led application delivery. The stakeholders here are developers and solution teams.
